## Notes

- Live fallback was not forced because Ollama was healthy and available.
- Live rate limiting was not enabled in the running container because
  `RATE_LIMIT_ENABLED=false` is the default.
- Live circuit breaker was not enabled in the running container because
  `CIRCUIT_BREAKER_FAILURE_THRESHOLD=0` is the default.
- The corresponding rate limit, concurrency, retry, and circuit breaker
  components were validated directly.
